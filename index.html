The Legend of Gooby

According to absolutely no scientific record, Gooby was discovered napping on a cloud during a mild windstorm and has been very confused ever since. It has the ears of a rabbit, the belly of a marshmallow, and the emotional range of a very good dog.

Gooby's favorite things, in order: carrots, being booped, belly jiggles, more carrots, and long naps between zoomies. This entire website exists to make Gooby happy. That's the whole plot.
